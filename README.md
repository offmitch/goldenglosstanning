# Golden Gloss Tanning

A responsive, client-focused website designed and developed for **Golden Gloss Tanning**, a Vancouver-area spray tanning business offering mobile and in-studio tanning services.

The website provides potential clients with information about available services, preparation and aftercare instructions, testimonials, a client gallery, and a consultation request form.

## Features

* Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile
* Custom branded visual design
* Single-page navigation with dynamic section views
* Mobile-friendly hamburger navigation
* Service information and pricing-ready service cards
* Client testimonial section
* Client photo gallery
* Interactive FAQ accordion
* Before and after-care information
* Consultation request form
* Conditional address field for mobile appointments
* Form validation and user feedback
* Dynamic years-of-experience calculation
* Dynamic copyright year
* Clickable phone, email, and Instagram contact links
* Accessible navigation and form elements
* Lazy-loaded gallery images

## Technologies Used

* **HTML5** — Semantic page structure and content
* **CSS3** — Responsive layouts, animations, styling, and custom branding
* **JavaScript** — Navigation, form interactions, validation, FAQ functionality, gallery interactions, and dynamic content
* **Google Fonts** — Custom typography
* **Font Awesome** — Social media icons

## Website Sections

### Home

Introduces Golden Gloss Tanning and highlights the company's tanning services, testimonials, and primary booking call-to-action.

### About Me

Introduces the owner and provides information about her experience, education, and approach to tanning services.

### Clients

A visual gallery showcasing client results and tanning work.

### Care + FAQ

Provides customers with before-care and after-care instructions along with answers to frequently asked questions.

### Book a Consultation

Allows potential clients to submit their contact information, preferred appointment type, date, time, and additional notes.

## Design

The website was designed around the Golden Gloss Tanning brand, using a warm, elegant aesthetic with:

* Blue and neutral tones
* Custom typography
* Large editorial-style headings
* Responsive layouts
* Subtle visual effects and transitions
* Image-focused sections
* Mobile-first navigation
* Clear calls to action

The goal was to create a polished experience that reflects the premium and personalized nature of the client's services while keeping the website easy to navigate.

## Responsive Design

The website is designed to adapt to different screen sizes and devices, including:

* Desktop computers
* Laptops
* Tablets
* Mobile phones

Navigation and content layouts adjust for smaller screens to maintain usability and readability.
